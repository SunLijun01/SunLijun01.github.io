Biography

孙丽俊，烟台大学计算机与控制工程学院软件工程专业，硕士在读。师从宋永超老师。研究方向为图像重建与计算机视觉， 侧重遥感图像处理、超分辨重建等。在IEEE Trans.期刊/中科院1区发表多篇论文。 主持烟台大学研究生科研创新基金项目。

Lijun Sun is currently a master's student at Yantai University, working with Prof. Yongchao Song (宋永超). She received her B.E. degree from Yantai University of School of Computer Science and Control Engineering. Her research interests include image processing, remote sensing and computer vision, mainly focusing on super-resolution tasks.
